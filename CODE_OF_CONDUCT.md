# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the users of the software

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Submitting deliberately malicious code or hidden backdoors
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

The project maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub.
